ABOUT NEXATECH

Technology Should Open Doors.

NexaTech Computer Literacy and Innovation Centre exists to help individuals and communities develop the digital knowledge, confidence and practical skills needed to participate meaningfully in a technology-driven world.

WHO WE ARE

Making Digital Skills More Accessible

Digital technology has become part of almost every aspect of modern life. Education, employment, communication, business and access to information increasingly depend on digital skills.

Yet access to technology alone is not enough. People need the knowledge and confidence to use technology effectively, responsibly and creatively.

NexaTech is designed as a learning and innovation environment where people can develop practical computer skills, explore emerging technologies and turn knowledge into useful solutions.

Our approach focuses on practical learning rather than simply understanding technology in theory. We believe people learn best when they can apply what they have learned to real situations.
