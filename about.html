ABOUT NEXATECH

Building Digital Confidence for the Future

NexaTech is a community-based computer literacy and innovation centre designed to bridge the digital skills gap within rural communities.

We provide practical training in computer literacy, digital productivity, programming, artificial intelligence and robotics while also offering essential ICT services to the community.

Our goal is to make technology accessible, practical and useful for people seeking to improve their education, employment opportunities and entrepreneurial potential.

Our Vision

To create a digitally empowered community where every member has access to quality digital training and the ability to innovate and thrive in a technology-driven world.

Our Mission

To provide accessible ICT training and innovation-focused digital skills that empower individuals for education, employment and entrepreneurship.
